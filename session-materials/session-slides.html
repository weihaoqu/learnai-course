PHASE 07 · POLISH · 70:00 → 80:00
PHASE 07 · POLISH

5 cheats to look pro.

  • Gradient background
  • One sans-serif font (Inter, Geist)
  • Lucide icons, not emojis
  • Generous whitespace
  • Dark mode by default

Tell Claude: "apply these."
