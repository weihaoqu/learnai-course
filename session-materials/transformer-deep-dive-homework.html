Tiny Glossary

TermMeaning for this courseYour example
TokenA chunk of text the model processes.
EmbeddingA numeric representation that places a token in a useful meaning space.
AttentionA mechanism that lets tokens use information from other tokens in context.
Transformer layerOne repeated block that updates token representations.
MLPA component that transforms representations and may help encode patterns or facts.
VerificationChecking an important claim against evidence outside fluent model output.
