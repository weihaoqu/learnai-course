AI Workflow Skill Map

Choose a practice by the problem you have—not by a slash-command menu. Commands are optional examples; judgment, evidence, privacy, and human approval are the learning objectives.

Student reference Vendor-neutral first Static-project boundary Updated August 20, 2026
Start with the lowest-power move that can answer the question. A conversation may be enough. Use a file-writing, publishing, committing, account-connected, or secret-handling workflow only when the task actually requires it and the instructor has approved the boundary.

1. Route by situation

Clarify

Ask for plain language, the missing premise, and one concrete example. Restate the idea yourself.

Stop: clarity is not verification
Decide

Surface assumptions, alternatives, criteria, and tradeoffs. Humans retain decisions and approval.

Stop: do not let AI invent the owner's answer
Investigate

Find where the answer lives: source material, another person, or a small design probe.

Stop: label unknowns and weak evidence
Build

Write a small behavior specification, slice vertically, and build the smallest reviewable path.

Stop: unclear behavior returns to decide
Verify

Define expected behavior, reproduce failures, test observable outcomes, review changes, and triage findings.

Stop: redact sensitive evidence
Transfer

Create a handoff only when work must move to another person, harness, directory, repository, or side task.

Stop: do not copy secrets or private data
Clarify→Locate answer→Define→Build small→Verify→Approve or revise

2. Where does the missing answer live?

The answer is in...Use this practiceStudent artifactDo not...
Your own judgmentRequirements interview or structured self-questioningDecision, criteria, and one rejected alternativeAgree automatically with the model's recommendation
Course or project materialSource-bound reading and document critiqueQuoted location, careful inference, and open questionTreat a summary as stronger than the original source
One other person's knowledgeSingle-recipient discovery questionnairePurpose, context, priority questions, and answer stubsAsk AI to guess policy, preference, or private facts
Nobody's knowledge yetDisposable design probeAssumption, observation, and unresolved questionPresent a mockup as production or research evidence
Observable system behaviorReproduction, test case, and controlled debuggingExpected versus actual result and verification recordChange several variables or expose private logs

3. Match authority to risk

Read, explain, or draft locally

Usually low-risk with allowed material. Still verify factual claims and disclose submitted assistance.

Write or change project files

Review the exact target, preserve unrelated work, inspect the diff, and test before acceptance.

Publish, commit, connect, or handle secrets

Requires explicit scope and approval. Use instructor guidance; never place credentials or private data in prompts.

No skill installed? Describe the practice in ordinary language. Example: “Ask me one question at a time until the user, boundary, success test, and main risk are clear. Recommend an answer, but wait for my decision.” Grading should measure the quality of the reasoning and artifact, not whether a command ran.
