## Live-moment features — when nicknames may be projected (NEW)

The redesigned deck has three live-moment features that involve student nicknames or counts:

1. **S6 nickname input** — Q types each student's chosen nickname into a `sessionStorage`-only field on slide S6. **Nicknames are never projected on any other slide by default.**
2. **S15 plan-check scoreboard** — Defaults to **count-only mode** (`N / 5 plans approved`). Q can opt into **named mode** by typing `:names on` during the session; this displays nicknames on S15. Default `:names off`.
3. **S22 fumble award** — Verbal-only. Q announces the funniest AI-bug story aloud at end of show & tell. **The student's nickname is NEVER displayed on the slide.**

### IRB caveat for `:names on` mode
Projecting student-supplied nicknames on a slide visible to other students and parents may exceed the scope of FA 2562's data-handling provisions. Before using `:names on` in a live session, **confirm with the Monmouth IRB office** that this fits within the approved protocol, or **file a protocol amendment**.

If unsure: stay in count-only mode. The pedagogy works either way.

## What NEVER changes regardless of consent status
- The student gets to do every activity.
- The student takes their built site home.
- The student receives the parent info sheet at the end.
- The student is not singled out, named, or treated differently.
