In-Class Baseline: Question 7

A student follows the course policy and discloses: “I used ChatGPT to brainstorm, outline, and rewrite my draft.” The final essay is much better than the student's usual writing. Another student writes alone and earns a lower grade. Is this fair?

Complete the class exit ticket without AI and retain it as your baseline. If you are absent or need an accommodation, complete the same prompt independently before using AI, or use an instructor-approved equivalent.

State your judgment, one condition that could change it, and what the grade should represent.

Memo Prompt

A. Diagnose the research-paper case ~150 words

Separate verified case facts, reading-supported claims, assumptions, and inferences. Explain how transparency, fairness, privacy, accountability, and safety appear in the case. Verified case facts are only facts stated in the assigned scenario; reading-supported claims are grounded in assigned readings; label your own reasonable extensions as inferences.

B. Challenge the ban-and-detector response ~200 words

Give the strongest ethical defense of a university ban and the strongest criticism of it. Apply at least two assigned reviews. Do not claim that these reviews establish detector accuracy, disparate impacts, classroom mistrust, concealment, or employment outcomes unless you have separately approved evidence.

C. Write an operational policy ~150–200 words

Write one policy paragraph that specifies permitted AI use, required disclosure, prohibited use, privacy and upload limits, how instructors verify learning, and the default when assignment instructions are silent. Avoid phrases such as “use AI responsibly” unless you define the required behavior.

D. Test your policy against Question 7 ~250–300 words

Explain why allowing AI can be fair and why it may still be unfair. Address equal rules, equitable access or capability, and assessment validity. State what the grade should measure, reach a final judgment, and identify one condition that would change your conclusion. If rewriting was explicitly permitted, explain where responsibility for any mismatch belongs.

AI Use for This Homework

The purpose of this assignment is to assess your ethical reasoning. The course therefore permits AI support for learning and feedback while reserving your claims, selected argument structure, submitted prose, and judgment for you.

Allowed with disclosure

  • Clarify a concept from an assigned reading.
  • Brainstorm alternative arguments or counterarguments.
  • Compare possible ways to organize claims you already developed; select and substantially revise the outline yourself.
  • Ask for feedback that identifies a problem, asks a question, or suggests a revision strategy.

Not allowed

  • Generate or rewrite sentences or paragraphs for submission.
  • Replace your analysis, judgment, or reading of the sources.
  • Invent, repair, or confirm a citation without checking the source.
  • Upload peer work, private course materials, personal data, or confidential information.
Privacy and evidence: Use only tools permitted by your instructor or university. You will not be required to surrender a private account, device, or full chat history. Your instructor may ask you to explain your reasoning or compare the memo with your in-class baseline.

AI-Use Disclosure not included in word count

Write approximately 50 words naming the tool, the purposes for which you used it, and the stages it affected. If you used no AI, write: “I did not use AI for this assignment.”
