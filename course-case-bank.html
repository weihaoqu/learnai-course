How To Use This Bank

For student modules

  • Use one hook slide, one discussion question, or one short reflection.
  • Promote only Week 11 career and Week 13 verification cases into heavier reading.
  • Keep technical posts as excerpts unless the class has the prerequisites.

For teacher prep

  • Use teacher-facing posts to design rubrics, examples, feedback workflows, and disclosure rules.
  • Reverify external sources before making numeric claims in class.
  • Favor cases that reinforce human judgment, not tool worship.
